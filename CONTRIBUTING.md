# Contributing

Thanks for helping improve **Awesome AI Software Testing**.

## What belongs here

Resources at the intersection of AI and software testing: test generation, test oracles and LLM-as-judge, self-healing automation, mutation testing, flaky-test detection, visual/AI UI testing, benchmarks, and work on the *trustworthiness* of AI-produced verdicts.

## Guidelines

- **Real and accurate.** The link must work and the description must be true. No vaporware, no affiliate links.
- **One line, neutral tone.** Describe what it is, not how amazing it is. No marketing copy.
- **Right section.** Place the entry under the most relevant heading; keep alphabetical-ish order within a section where practical.
- **Notable.** Prefer resources that are widely used, peer-reviewed, or clearly substantive over brand-new/unproven ones.
- **No duplicates.** Check it isn't already listed.

## How to submit

1. Fork the repo.
2. Add your entry in the format:
   `- [Name](https://link) — Short neutral description.`
3. Open a pull request explaining why it belongs and, for research, the venue/year.

Maintainer review focuses on accuracy and relevance. Low-quality or promotional entries will be declined.
